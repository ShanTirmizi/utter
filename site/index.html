Let go

Your Mac turns the audio into text, tidies it, and pastes it where you were typing.

It stays on your Mac

Speech is transcribed on your own machine by an open speech model. Audio is never uploaded and never kept. There is no account, no analytics and no telemetry.

Your key, or none

Cleanup is optional. Bring a key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter, point it at any OpenAI-compatible service, or run a local model through Ollama with no key at all. Without any of these you get the raw transcription.

Tidy, or rewrite

One hotkey keeps your wording and removes the ums, repeats and false starts. A second one rewrites what you said so it reads better: clearer, shorter, more professional, friendlier, or by an instruction you write yourself.

Install

  1. Download the DMG, open it, and drag Utter into Applications.
  2. Open Utter. macOS will refuse the first time. Go to System Settings, then Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to the message about Utter.
  3. Follow the welcome screens: allow the microphone, switch on Accessibility so Utter can paste for you, and let the speech model download (148 MB).

Why macOS objects

Apps from outside the App Store are expected to be signed with a paid Apple Developer ID. Utter does not have one yet, so macOS cannot tell you who made it. The whole source is public, and you can build it yourself instead. Two things follow from the missing signature:

  • You have to approve the first launch by hand, as above.
  • After each update, macOS asks for the Microphone and Accessibility permissions again.

If you prefer Terminal, this one command approves the app instead of step 2:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Utter.app

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 12 or later. Intel Macs are not supported by this build. To build from source, see the README.

What leaves your Mac

Audio Never.
Text Only if you set up cleanup with a hosted provider. Then the transcript and the name of the app you are typing in go to that provider, using your key. With a local model they stay on your Mac.
Keys Stored in the macOS Keychain and sent only to the provider each one belongs to.
Speech models Downloaded once from Hugging Face, when you ask.
Anything else Nothing. Utter has no account, no analytics and no telemetry.
