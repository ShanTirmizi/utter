One hotkey tidies. The other rewrites.

You choose sentence by sentence, by which keys you hold. Both can be changed, and dictation can be the fn key on its own.

control option Space

Tidy

Keeps your wording. Removes the ums, repeats and false starts, fixes the punctuation, and breaks a long dictation into short paragraphs.

Rewrite

Rewrites what you said so it reads better, in the style you picked or by an instruction you wrote yourself. Short paragraphs, and lists where you named several things.

Tidying and rewriting are done by a language model you set up. Without one, Utter types the raw transcription. How cleanup works

It writes for the app you are in.

Utter checks which app is in front and shapes the text to suit. A chat message, an email and a code comment each come out differently.

Kind of appHow the text is written
Chat Slack, Messages, Discord Relaxed and conversational. No greeting or sign-off is added, and separate thoughts go on separate lines.
Email Mail, Outlook, Gmail Complete sentences in short paragraphs. A greeting stays if you said one.
Code editor or terminal Xcode, VS Code, Terminal Technical terms, commands, file names and identifiers spelled exactly. No Markdown.
Notes Notes, Obsidian, Pages Clean prose in full sentences. When you list several things, you get a list.
AI assistant Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini A clear, direct prompt that keeps every detail you gave, in your order.

In a browser, Utter recognises sites such as Gmail, Slack, Claude and ChatGPT and writes for them. Anywhere else, you get plain, neutral prose.

Ready when you are

The microphone is kept ready between dictations, without listening, so it starts the moment you press and your first word is less likely to be clipped.

Cleanup is optional. The key is yours.

A language model can tidy or rewrite each dictation. Whether one does, and which one, is up to you.

Nothing set up

You get the raw transcription, typed as it was heard.

Text stays on your Mac

Your own key

Add a key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter, or point Utter at any OpenAI-compatible service.

Text goes to the provider you chose

A local model

Run a model on your Mac through Ollama. It needs no key at all.

Text stays on your Mac

If cleanup fails for any reason, Utter types what it heard and tells you why.

What leaves your Mac

Speech is transcribed on your Mac by an open speech model, whisper.cpp, running on the GPU. The audio is never uploaded and never kept.

Audio
Never.
It is transcribed on your Mac and then discarded.
Text
Only if you set it up.
With cleanup by a hosted provider, the transcript, the name of the app or site and your word list go to that provider, with your key. So does text you select to edit. With a local model, all of it stays on your Mac.
History
Never.
It is one file on your Mac, readable only by your account. Clear it or switch it off.
Keys
Only to their own provider.
They are stored in the macOS Keychain. Each one is sent only to the provider it belongs to.
Speech models
Downloaded once.
Each model is fetched from Hugging Face the first time you choose it.
Anything else
Nothing.
No account, no analytics, no telemetry.

Install

You need a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 12 or later.

Download for Mac

  1. Download the DMG

    Open it and drag Utter into Applications.

  2. Open Utter

    It is signed and notarised by Apple, so it opens like any other app.

  3. Follow the welcome screens

    Allow the microphone, switch on Accessibility so Utter can paste for you, and let the speech model download.

Signed and notarised

Every release is signed with a Developer ID and checked by Apple before it reaches you. Two things follow.

  • macOS opens it without any extra steps.
  • Your Microphone and Accessibility permissions carry over from one update to the next.

Or build it yourself

The source is all there. The README lists the tools you need.
